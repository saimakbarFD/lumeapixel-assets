What we do

Base. Pour. Finish.

Everything is poured by our own crew, not brokered out to whoever is free that week.

The base

Old slab out and hauled away. Graded aggregate compacted in lifts, steel set on chairs. The day nobody photographs.

The pour

Forms set to the fall we agreed, then placed, screeded and floated in one continuous pour by our own crew.

The finish

Control joints cut the same day to depth, curing and sealing explained, and the site washed down before we leave.

How it works

How your project runs

Concrete is a one-shot material. Everything that decides how it ages happens before and during a single day.

01

We measure on site

We walk the job, check the fall, look at where the water goes, and send a written price with each part on its own line.

02

Tear-out, base, then the pour

The old slab goes on a truck. Graded aggregate is compacted in lifts, forms and steel go in, then one continuous pour.

03

Joint, cure, clean

Control joints cut the same day, the site washed down, and clear instructions on when you can walk and drive on it.

Who we are

Metro Atlanta's concrete crew, from the base up

Concrete Xperts is a locally owned concrete contractor working across metro Atlanta: driveways, patios, walkways, stamped and decorative work, retaining walls and commercial slabs. The same crew pours it and the same standard applies.

Most concrete in Georgia does not fail because of the concrete. It fails because of what was underneath it. Red clay holds water, swells, and shrinks back in a dry August. We spend the first day on the part nobody photographs.
