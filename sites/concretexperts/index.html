How it works

Four steps, and one of them matters most.

Concrete is a one-shot material. Everything that decides how it ages happens before and during a single day.

01

We measure on site

We walk the job, check the fall, look at where the water goes, and send a written price with each part on its own line.

02

Tear-out and base

The old slab comes out and goes on a truck. Then graded aggregate, compacted in lifts, the day that decides the next ten years.

03

Form, steel, pour

Forms set to the fall we agreed, mesh or rebar up on chairs, then placed, screeded and floated in one continuous pour.

04

Joint, cure, clean

Control joints cut the same day to the right depth, the site washed down, and clear instructions on when you can walk and drive on it.
