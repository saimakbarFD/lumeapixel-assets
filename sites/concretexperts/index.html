How your project runs

Concrete is a one-shot material. Everything that decides how it ages happens before and during a single day.

01

We measure on site

02

03

Before you call

Straight answers on price

Concrete is quoted by the square foot, and that number on its own tells you almost nothing. Our written quote lists tear-out, base, reinforcement, thickness and haul-off separately, so you can hold it next to anyone else's and compare like with like.

Ask us: (404) 474-2999
How much does a concrete driveway cost?+

It is priced by the square foot, but the rate depends on thickness, reinforcement, how much demolition and haul-off comes first, and whether a truck can reach the pour. A flat drive a mixer can back onto and a steep one that has to be barrowed are very different jobs at the same size. We measure on site and put it in writing.

How do I choose a concrete contractor in Atlanta?+

Ask every contractor the same four things: how thick, and with what reinforcement; what they do about the base, which on Georgia red clay is most of the job; when the control joints get cut; and for a certificate of insurance before anyone starts. The written quote should show demolition and haul-off as their own line.

Why do you price after a site visit?+

Access decides whether a truck can reach the pour or whether it has to be barrowed, and that single fact can change the labor on a job more than the size does. It costs you nothing to have us come and look.

Do you pour stamped concrete?+

Yes. We pour stamped concrete across metro Atlanta: patios, walkways, pool decks, driveways and stamped overlays on sound existing slabs, with the color mixed through the concrete on new pours and the joints planned into the pattern.
