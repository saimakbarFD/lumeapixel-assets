The Tampa Concrete Works difference

Good concrete starts below the surface.

A written scope and clear pricing before work begins.

Tampa properties put concrete through a lot: afternoon thunderstorms from June through September, a high water table near the bay, and sandy fill soils that can shift when drainage isn't planned correctly.

That's why our work starts with a site evaluation that checks grade, compaction, and where water will go once the slab is in place. Every pour gets the right base, reinforcement, and joint placement for Hillsborough County conditions.

Commercial projects

Keep the job moving with a defined scope.

Work around a commercial property needs planning. Access routes, active tenants, delivery schedules, removal areas, and jobsite coordination all matter. We review the scope with the people responsible for the site and keep communication direct from planning through completion.

We work from available plans and field conditions to define the areas to be removed, prepared, formed, poured, and finished. If a site condition affects the work, we raise it early.
