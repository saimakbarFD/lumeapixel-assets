On site

6,200 sq ft of overhead concrete repair at Zion Hill.

The Atlanta Branch of Western Specialty Contractors restores the concrete structures that people drive, park and worship in every day. Whether the job is a university garage or a church deck, we plan the work around the owner's timeline and the engineer's requirements.

When a specified method won't work in the field, our crews test it, explain why, and propose a better one. On the KSU West Deck, that meant re-tensioning barrier cables with turnbuckles instead of pulling them from the end caps.

Recent work

Two recent Atlanta Branch projects, from a university campus to a historic church.

Kennesaw, GA

KSU West Parking Garage

General maintenance, repairs and waterproofing at Kennesaw State University's West Deck, 48 Campus Loop Road. Work covered concrete repairs, epoxy injection, and routing and sealing of the deck surface. When the specified method risked pulling end caps out of the columns, the crew re-tensioned the barrier cables with turnbuckles instead. Hired by Ian Tierney-Walker Consultants; engineer of record Walker Consultants.

Atlanta, GA

Zion Hill Parking Garage

Concrete restoration at Zion Hill Baptist Church, 6175 Campbellton Road SW. The hardest part was the overhead slab, with 6,200 sq ft of repairs completed on a short owner timeline. Attaching chipping hammers to air jacks let the crew keep production moving.
