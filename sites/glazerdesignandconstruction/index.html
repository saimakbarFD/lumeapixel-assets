FAQ

Questions, answered

Not seeing your question? Call (404) 683-9848 or email info@glazerconstruction.com.

What kinds of projects does Glazer take on?

Kitchen and bathroom remodeling, custom homes, interior and home renovations, outdoor living spaces, handicap accessible homes and commercial construction.

Which areas do you serve?

We work in Atlanta and nearby communities including Brookhaven, Buckhead, Sandy Springs, Alpharetta, Decatur, Dunwoody, Marietta, Roswell, Vinings, Virginia Highland, Chastain Park and Midtown.

Do you design as well as build?

Yes. Glazer Design and Construction follows a design and build process, so design and construction are handled together.

Do you offer green construction?

Green construction is part of our work. Ask us about it when you call for your estimate.

How do I get an estimate?

Call (404) 683-9848, email info@glazerconstruction.com or send a request through the contact form below.
