About ImproveIT!

Locally owned. Roofing is all we do.

ImproveIT! of Atlanta is a locally owned and operated company offering residential roofing. Our team has a combined 40+ years of experience in roof repair, replacement, insurance restoration and storm damage repair.

We have replaced roofs for over 7,000 homeowners. As a factory-certified GAF and CertainTeed contractor, we install quality materials with craftsmanship you can count on.

Insurance claims

Storm damage? We work with your adjuster.

If wind, hail or a storm has hit your roof, we help you file the claim and get the compensation your project needs. We have licensed insurance adjusters on staff to help minimize your out-of-pocket expenses.

We partner with your insurance adjuster through every step, from the first inspection to the finished roof.
