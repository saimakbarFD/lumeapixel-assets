About

Locally owned,
part of your community

Kitchen Tune-Up New Braunfels is run by owners Rudy & Debra Jimenez. Our team lives in your community and understands what homeowners here need from their kitchens.

We ask what colors and styles you like, and which features and functions you want. Then we work toward an update that matches, at an investment you are comfortable with. A national franchise system backs us, so whatever your style, we can help you find the solution.

Our work

Real kitchens,
used every day

The projects we show are real remodeling jobs in homes like yours, not designer showrooms. One New Braunfels kitchen went from oak doors, a two-tiered granite island and beige tile to white shaker doors, quartz countertops, a glass backsplash and a leveled island for a more open gathering space.

Another started with oak doors and black granite and ended with white shaker uppers, black base cabinets, new granite and an accent tile backsplash.
