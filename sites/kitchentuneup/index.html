About us

A local team that works with you on your kitchen

At Kitchen Tune-Up New Braunfels, we ask what colors and styles you like, and what features and functions you want. Then we build a plan that matches your desires at an investment you are comfortable with.

We are locally owned and operated, we live in your community, and we are backed by a national franchise system. Whether your wood cabinets need a Tune-Up or you want a new layout and redesign, we make the process enjoyable and hassle-free.

Cabinet renovations

A new look without a full tear-out

Cabinet renovations can change the look and feel of a kitchen without a complete overhaul. Refacing replaces the door fronts and keeps the existing cabinet boxes. Painting gives tired cabinets a new color on a tighter budget.

Custom cabinetry makes every inch work harder, with more storage and a layout built around how you cook and gather.
