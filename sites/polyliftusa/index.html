Foamjection technology

Compact. Stabilize. Lift.

Foamjection is designed to compact weak or sinking subgrade, stabilize the slab above it and, if needed, lift the slab to remove uneven surfaces and trip hazards.

Compact the subgrade

Weak soil is the root cause of sinking and cracking. Foam expands under pressure to compact it.

Stabilize the slab

Pressure gauges tell the installer when the subgrade is fully compacted and the slab is stable.

Lift to level

Extra foam is injected slowly to raise the slab as close as possible to its original position.

How it works

How your project runs

Four steps, one visit. Most projects are completed in less than a day.

1

Drill

Penny-size holes are drilled through the slab where the damage exists, about five feet apart.

2

Inject

Ports are installed and hot liquid polyurethane foam is injected under pressure, compacting the subgrade.

3

Lift

If lift is desired, more foam goes in very slowly to raise the slab back toward its original position.

4

Finish

Injection sites are filled and leveled with sanded concrete, and the work area is cleaned up.

The slab is ready for use within one hour of completion.
