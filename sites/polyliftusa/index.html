How it works

Foamjection, step by step

Foamjection compacts weak or sinking subgrade, stabilizes the slab above and, if needed, lifts it to remove unlevel surfaces and trip hazards.

01

Drill

Penny-size holes are drilled through the slab where the damage exists, about five feet apart.

02

Inject

Ports are installed and hot liquid polyurethane foam is injected under pressure. It expands to compact the subgrade and stabilize the slab.

03

Gauge

Pressure gauges tell the installer when the subgrade is fully compacted and the slab is stabilized.

04

Lift

If lift is desired, more foam is injected very slowly to raise the slab as close as possible to its original position.

05

Patch

The injection sites are filled and leveled using sanded concrete.

06

Clean up

The work area is cleaned. Most projects are done in less than a day, and the slab is ready for use within one hour.

FAQ

Common questions

What is concrete leveling?+

A cost-effective way to lift and level sunken, uneven or cracked concrete. Polyurethane foam is injected under the slab to fill voids, stabilize the soil and raise the concrete back to its original position, with no costly replacement.

How long does it take?+

Most residential projects like driveways, sidewalks or patios are completed in just a few hours. The foam cures in about half an hour, so you can walk or drive on the surface the same day.

Is the foam eco-friendly?+

Yes. Our polyurethane foam is EPA-tested and approved, uses minimal material, produces little waste and avoids removing and replacing old concrete.

How does the process work?+

First we compact the subgrade underneath the slab, the root cause of sinking and cracking. Next we stabilize the slab, then lift it to reduce unlevel surfaces and trip hazards. Finally we patch the holes with sanded concrete.
