Let's take a moment to think about dust

Your family needs protection.

Bath remodeling is a very dusty process. Dust, mildew, mold, silica and other hazards can come out of a newly demoed bath. Sealing the room off keeps them from being spread through your house by the HVAC system.

We use the Dust Defense Containment System on every bath remodel. It gives your home 360 degrees of protection and gives us direct access from the entrance to the project room, from setup to completion.

Even for something as simple as a bathtub replacement, runners and plastic sheeting aren't enough.
