Dust Defense System

A dust-free remodel is a big deal

Bath remodeling is a dusty process. Demolition can release mildew, mold, silica and other contaminants, and plastic sheeting and runners aren't enough.

We use the Dust Defense Containment System on all of our projects. It seals the project bathroom off from the rest of your home, giving your family 360 degrees of protection and giving our crew direct access from the entrance to the room.
