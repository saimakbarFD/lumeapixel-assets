Questions, answered

What does the process look like?+

You meet with Michael to go over the scope of work. A detailed quote and general timeline follow, then a pre-demo meeting confirms what needs to be ordered before work begins.

Who helps with selections?+

Peacock Alley works with a designer to finalize hardware, wall coverings, lighting and other selections. They also help coordinate orders from different vendors.

When does the work start?+

The job doesn’t begin until all the materials are gathered and ready for installation, which keeps the project moving once demo starts.

Can we stay in the home during a remodel?+

One client with an extensive interior and exterior remodel of their South Tampa home was able to stay in the house throughout, thanks to the team’s flexibility.

What kinds of projects do you take on?+

Kitchens, bathrooms, custom built-ins, bar areas, flooring, painting, backsplash, lighting and fireplace renovations, plus larger interior and exterior remodels around Tampa Bay.
