How it works

Six steps from first call to finished room

01

Scope meeting

We meet at your home with Michael to walk through what you want changed and what the work involves.

02

Quote and timeline

You receive a detailed quote and a general timeline before anything is agreed.

03

Pre-demo meeting

We confirm what needs to be ordered and review the plan together before demolition begins.

04

Selections

Our designer helps you finalize hardware, lighting, wall coverings and other finishes.

05

Materials gathered

Work starts once materials are on hand and ready for installation, so the job runs without stalls.

06

Build and walkthrough

Michael and Dan stay involved through the build, and we resolve any issues that come up before we call it done.
