How to get started

Four steps, no surprises.

We believe in total transparency before a single shovel hits the ground. Here is how every Titan project runs.

01

Consultation

It starts with a conversation. We learn your vision and look at any photos you have to understand the scope, then book a site visit that fits your calendar.

02

On-site inspection

A free inspection at your job site. We measure, photograph and evaluate terrain, drainage and soil so the installation lasts.

03

Detailed proposal

A comprehensive estimate by email, followed by a personal call to answer questions and walk through the details.

04

Precision execution

Once approved, our team works with quality materials and experienced labor. We are licensed and insured, and your property stays protected.
