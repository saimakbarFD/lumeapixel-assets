The Berndsen Company

Atlanta luxury homes, built to the last detail

The Berndsen Company is a premier custom builder of bespoke Atlanta residences. From classical stone and plaster homes to timber retreats and high-rise condominiums, every project is built around how our clients live.

Our work has earned the Greater Atlanta Home Builders Association OBIE Award nine times, and the ICAA's 2008 Shutze Award for the David Residence, built with Historical Concepts.

Our process

One builder, from first drawing to final walkthrough

We work alongside architects, designers and homeowners from the start, so decisions about structure, materials and finishes are made together and built the way they were drawn.

Whether the project is a new residence, a floor in a high-rise or a covered terrace with a fire pit, you deal with the same team throughout.
