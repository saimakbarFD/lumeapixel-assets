Condominiums

About The Berndsen Co

Luxury homes, built the Atlanta way

The Berndsen Company is a luxury home builder in Atlanta, creating bespoke residences with classical detail and modern comfort. Our work has been recognized by the Greater Atlanta Home Builders Association and the ICAA, and featured in leading design publications.
