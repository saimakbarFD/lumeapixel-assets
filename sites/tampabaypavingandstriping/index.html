FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Call (813) 710-6038 and we'll talk it through.

How much does a quote cost?+

Nothing. Our quotes are 100% free and done in person.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes. Tampa Bay Paving and Striping is licensed, insured and bonded.

How long does resurfacing take?+

Most Tampa resurfacing projects wrap up in a day or two, depending on the size.

How often should a parking lot be resealed?+

We recommend resealing a parking lot every 2 to 3 years to stay ahead of problems. Fresh sealcoat also gives striping a darker, cleaner surface to stand out against.

Do you work on both homes and businesses?+

Yes. We handle residential and commercial paving, resurfacing, sealing and repair, plus concrete curbing, sidewalks and driveways.

How do you fix potholes?+

We don't just fill them in. We repair the base if needed and compact new asphalt for a lasting fix.
