Common questions

What Tampa homeowners ask us

Licensed Florida Certified Building Contractor, license CBC1269188.

How do I verify that Novacore Builders is licensed in Florida? +

Check directly on the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation website at myfloridalicense.com. Search for license number CBC1269188 to see our active status, license type and coverage area. We encourage every homeowner to check before hiring any contractor, including us.

How much does a home remodel cost in Tampa, St. Pete and Clearwater? +

Costs vary with the size of the project, materials and design. After your consultation we provide a detailed written estimate so you can see exactly where your budget is going.

Why is hiring a licensed contractor important? +

It helps ensure your remodel is legal, safe, insured and built to last, protecting your home and its resale value.

Do you also build custom homes? +

Yes. Along with remodeling and additions, Novacore Builders is a licensed custom home builder serving Tampa, St. Pete and Clearwater, guiding you from design and permitting through the final walkthrough.
