FAQ

Questions Tampa property owners ask

What paving services do you offer?+

Asphalt driveway installation, concrete driveway services, commercial paving, asphalt sealcoating, asphalt repair, parking lot striping and asphalt milling, for homes and businesses.

How do I get a free quote?+

Call (813) 518-8316 or send us your details through the contact form. We will get back to you promptly to talk through your project.

How long does a paving project take?+

Parking lot striping and sealcoating are usually done within a day or two. Residential driveways typically take a few days to a week. Repairs depend on the damage, and commercial timelines depend on size and complexity. Call us for an estimate on your project.

Should I repair, resurface or replace?+

Minor cracks and potholes can often be patched. Resurfacing fits worn pavement with a stable base. Full replacement is usually best when there is widespread structural failure, drainage problems or a deteriorated foundation. We inspect the property and recommend the option with the best long-term value.

What does asphalt paving cost in Tampa?+

It depends on project size, pavement thickness, access, grading, drainage, base preparation and the condition of the existing surface. We price after evaluating your property, based on the actual scope of work.
