What goes on your roof

Five layers. Zero shortcuts.

What goes down on a Mosaic replacement, and what most quotes quietly skip.

  1. 01

    Ridge vent + Attic Armor

    Attic Armor goes in directly under the ridge vent during the roofing process, sealing the gap against pests without blocking ventilation.

  2. 02

    Architectural shingles

    GAF and Owens Corning, installed to manufacturer spec by a GAF Certified contractor.

  3. 03

    Drip edge + flashing

    New, not reused. Every edge, valley and penetration re-flashed.

  4. 04

    Synthetic underlayment

    Better than felt paper against wind-driven rain and Atlanta's summer heat.

  5. 05

    Roof decking

    Every soft or damaged sheet replaced up to the decking allowance in your written contract. More is quoted in writing first, with photos.
