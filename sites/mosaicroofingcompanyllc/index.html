What goes on your roof

Five layers. Zero shortcuts.

A roof is a system, not a shingle. Here is what goes down on a Mosaic replacement, and what most quotes quietly skip.

  • Ridge vent and Attic Armor

    Sealed against pests without blocking ventilation.

  • Architectural shingles

    GAF and Owens Corning, installed to manufacturer spec by a GAF Certified contractor.

  • Drip edge and flashing

    New, not reused. Every edge, valley and penetration re-flashed.

  • Synthetic underlayment

    Better than felt paper against wind-driven rain and Atlanta summer heat.

  • Roof decking

    Every soft or damaged sheet replaced and photographed.
