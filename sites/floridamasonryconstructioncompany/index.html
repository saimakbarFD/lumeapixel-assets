FAQ

Questions, answered

Need something else? Call (813) 626-4518 and we will talk it through.

What kind of projects do you build? +

We specialize in new commercial concrete tilt wall construction projects, including foundations and the tilt wall concrete construction itself.

Why choose tilt wall construction? +

Tilt wall is generally considered seismic resistant and more resistant to moisture, because there are no mortar joints throughout the wall like there are with CMU block walls.

How long have you been in business? +

We have been building in Florida since 1965. We are a well-established family-owned business, and our customers keep coming back and referring us to others.

Are you part of an industry association? +

Yes. We are a member of the Tilt-Up Concrete Association.

How do you handle safety? +

We are committed to a safe work environment. Safety is a priority on our job sites and in everything we do.
