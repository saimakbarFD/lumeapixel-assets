About us

Value, creative design and attention to detail

Marc Anthony Builders is an award-winning Plan, Design, Build custom builder serving the Tampa Bay area. We specialize in new custom homes, home additions and home remodeling.

Building a home is an intimate connection between dreams, concepts and reality. That is why our clients work closely with the architect, project manager and designer. We believe the relationship with the customer comes before the design and construction of the home.
