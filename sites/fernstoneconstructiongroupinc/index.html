About Fernstone

A general contractor headquartered in Atlanta

Fernstone Construction Group, Inc. is a full-service general contracting company focused on residential building and design. We specialize in turnkey complete home renovations, custom kitchen and bathroom buildouts and capital improvement projects.

Our team prides itself on delivering projects on time and on budget, exceeding client expectations with superior skills, craftsmanship and communication. We work closely with you throughout the process so your vision becomes a reality.
