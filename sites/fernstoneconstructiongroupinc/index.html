FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Fernstone build? +

We are a full-service general contractor focused on residential building and design. We specialize in turnkey complete home renovations, custom kitchen and bathroom buildouts and capital improvement projects.

Where do you work? +

We are headquartered in Atlanta, GA and serve clients in Alabama, Florida, Georgia and North Carolina.

Are you licensed and insured? +

Yes. Fernstone Construction Group is licensed, bonded and insured in four states.

How much experience does your team have? +

Our team brings more than 25 years of combined experience in residential construction, and we have completed over 1,000 full-scale property flips.

How do I get started? +

Call (678) 977-0043 or send us your project address and a short description through the contact form. We work closely with you throughout the process so your vision becomes a reality.
