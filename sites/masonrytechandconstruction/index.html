What we do

Masonry, hardscape and restoration

From a new patio to a hundred-year-old brick wall, the same crew handles the stone, the brick and the details.

Hardscape

Paver patios, stone seating walls, steps, walkways and limestone edging for your yard.

Masonry & construction

Brick walls, sidewalks, block work and stone built to match the home around them.

Restoration

Repointing and repair of old common bond brick and stone walls on historic buildings.
