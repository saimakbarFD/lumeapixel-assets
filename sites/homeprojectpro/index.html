Meet the owner

Mike Wellington

Originally from the suburbs of Chicago, Mike grew up helping his father with home projects and basement remodels, which gave him a knack for working with his hands. He spent many years in the home improvement industry, building the skills needed for a wide range of repairs.

After moving to Denver in 2009, he worked in real estate and became familiar with different construction principles and build styles. When the chance came, he started his handyman business. After years of success, he followed his dream of moving to Florida and bringing his services to the Sunshine State.
