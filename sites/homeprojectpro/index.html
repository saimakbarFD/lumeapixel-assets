About

Mike Wellington, owner

Originally from the suburbs of Chicago, Mike grew up helping his father with home projects and basement remodels. He spent years in the home improvement industry refining the skills behind a wide range of repairs, and later worked in real estate in Denver, which made him familiar with different construction principles and build styles.

After years of success running his handyman business, Mike followed his dream and moved to Florida to bring his services to the Sunshine State.
