About Premier Concrete

The work is in what you can't see

Charlotte's clay soil swells when it is wet and shrinks when it dries. That movement is the most common reason slabs crack, settle and heave. We dig deep enough to clear unstable topsoil, then build on a compacted gravel base that drains and stays put.

Rebar or fiber mesh adds strength, and control joints guide any cracking to planned lines instead of random fractures across your driveway or patio. It is the same careful process on every job, big or small.

Charlotte know-how

Permits, HOAs and the right season

Many concrete projects in Charlotte need a permit, and driveways that meet a public street typically need a driveway permit. If you live in a planned community, your HOA may require approval before work starts. We help prepare the paperwork, including site plans, material specs and finish samples, so you avoid fines and forced removal.

Timing matters too. Summer heat dries a pour too fast, so we schedule early mornings and use curing methods that hold moisture. Spring and fall are ideal. Permits can take days to weeks, so plan ahead for spring and early summer.
