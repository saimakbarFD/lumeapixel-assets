Our approach

Clay moves.
Your slab shouldn't.

Clay-heavy soil swells when it's wet and shrinks when it dries. Without a proper base, that movement turns into cracks, settling and heaving. It's the most common reason concrete fails early in this area, and it's preventable.

We excavate deep enough to clear unstable topsoil, install 4 to 6 inches of compacted crushed stone, add rebar or fiber reinforcement, and place control joints so any cracking lands where it's meant to.

Before you pour

Permits and HOA approval, sorted first.

Driveways that connect to a public street typically need a driveway permit, and larger patios or attached structures may need a full building permit. Skipping permits can mean fines, forced removal, or trouble when you sell.

Many Charlotte neighborhoods also require HOA pre-approval for exterior concrete. We help prepare the submittal, with site plans, material specs and finish samples, so nothing gets torn out later. Permits take time, so plan ahead, especially for spring and early summer.

  • Driveway and building permits
  • HOA architectural review packages
  • Seasonal pour scheduling for heat and cold
