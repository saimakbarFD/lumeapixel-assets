What we do

Prepare. Pour. Finish.

Concrete lasts when the work before the truck arrives is done right. That's where we start.

Prepare

Excavate past unstable topsoil, then compact 4 to 6 inches of crushed stone for drainage and a stable base.

Pour

Rebar or fiber reinforcement, formed control joints, and pours timed around heat and cold.

Finish

Broom finish or stamped patterns, then curing methods that protect the surface while it gains strength.

What Charlotte concrete needs

Clay soil

Clay swells when wet and shrinks when dry. Without a compacted gravel base, that movement shows up as cracks, settling and heaving.

Permits

Driveways tied to a public street and larger patios or foundations can need permits. Plan for lead time, especially in spring and early summer.

HOA review

Many Charlotte neighborhoods require pre-approval for width, finish and placement. We help prepare site plans and material specs for the submittal.

Season and weather

Summer heat calls for early pours and curing compounds. In winter, fresh concrete must be protected from freezing. Spring and fall are ideal.
