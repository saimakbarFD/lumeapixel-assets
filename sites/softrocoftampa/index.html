Why rubber

Plain concrete or Softroc.

Traditional concrete

  • Gets hot in direct sun
  • Can be slick when wet
  • Hard and unforgiving underfoot
  • Limited to the plain gray look

Softroc rubber surfacing

  • Stays cooler underfoot
  • Grips when wet and drains quickly
  • Shock-absorbing, comfortable to walk and play on
  • Fade-resistant color in any blend you want

Multiple color options

Pick the colors that match your style.

Softroc uses fade-resistant technology for a better long-term appearance on pool decks, driveways, sidewalks, splash pads and more.

Each rubber granule is colored all the way through, so it's easy to cut and inset a different color to match your design. We can create any color you like or mix your own blend for a speckled look.
