Multiple color options

Pick the colors that match your style.

Softroc uses fade-resistant technology for a better long-term appearance. Each rubber granule is colored all the way through, so it's easier to cut and insert a different-colored rubber to meet your design. We can also mix your own color blends for a speckled look.

Why rubber

Plain concrete vs. Softroc.

Traditional concrete

  • Gets hot underfoot in the summer sun
  • Slick when wet around pools and splash areas
  • Water can sit after heavy afternoon storms
  • Hard surface with no give

Softroc rubber surfacing

  • Stays cooler underfoot
  • Slip-resistant when wet
  • Drains fast, so puddles don't linger
  • UV resistant and fade-resistant color
  • Shock-absorbing and low maintenance
