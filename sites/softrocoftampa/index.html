Why rubber

Plain concrete or Softroc.

Traditional concrete

  • Gets hot in the Florida sun
  • Slick when splashed or wet
  • Puddles linger after storms
  • Hard surface with no give underfoot

Softroc rubber surfacing

  • Stays cooler underfoot
  • Slip-resistant when wet
  • Drains fast so puddles don't linger
  • UV-resistant, fade-resistant color

Color options

Pick the colors that match your style.

Softroc uses fade-resistant technology for a better long-term look on your pool deck, driveway, sidewalk, splash pad and more. Each rubber granule is colored all the way through, so it is easy to cut and inlay a different color to your design.

We can also create any color you like, or mix your own blends for a speckled look.

  • Color all the way through each granule
  • Custom colors and speckled blends
  • Inlays cut to your design
