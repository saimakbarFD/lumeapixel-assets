Frequently asked questions

Straight answers

Where do you work?+

Double D Concrete is based in Atlanta, Georgia, and provides residential and commercial paving and concrete services throughout Metro Atlanta and surrounding communities.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. We provide straightforward, no-pressure consultations and upfront estimates based on the exact specifications of your residential or commercial project.

How long does a driveway take?+

Most residential driveway installations take 1 to 3 days depending on site preparation and weather, followed by standard curing time before vehicle access.

Do you handle both concrete and asphalt?+

Yes. Our crew of 10 is equipped for concrete installations and repairs as well as asphalt paving, resurfacing and maintenance.

What experience do you bring?+

Over 10 years of field experience and solid workmanship on residential and commercial projects.

How do you prepare the site?+

Proper prep is vital for durability. We handle site grading, soil compaction and sturdy sub-base installation so your concrete or asphalt won't crack or settle early.
