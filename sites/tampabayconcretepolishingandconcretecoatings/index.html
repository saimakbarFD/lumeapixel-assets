FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

What is the difference between polished concrete and epoxy flooring?+

Polished concrete enhances your existing slab by mechanically grinding and polishing it, while epoxy adds a protective coating over the surface. Polished concrete offers a natural finish with minimal maintenance. Epoxy provides additional chemical resistance, color options and decorative finishes.

Do you handle surface prep and repairs?+

Yes. We handle grinding, crack repair and full prep to ensure a strong, long-lasting finish.

What types of concrete floor coatings do you install?+

We install epoxy, polyaspartic, polyurea, urethane, metallic epoxy, flake broadcast, quartz broadcast, anti-slip and chemical-resistant floor coating systems for commercial, industrial and residential properties throughout Florida.

How long do concrete floor coatings last?+

A professionally installed coating can last 10 to 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Lifespan depends on the coating type, traffic levels and how the floor is used.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve commercial, industrial and residential customers throughout Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Lakeland, Sarasota, Bradenton, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel, New Port Richey and surrounding Florida communities.
