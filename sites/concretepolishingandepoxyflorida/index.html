What we do

Every floor starts with the right preparation. From there, we match the system to how the space is used.

Why a specialist

Bare concrete vs. a finished floor

Leaving concrete as is

  • Uneven, tired-looking surface
  • Spills and stains soak in
  • Wear and abrasion in high-traffic areas
  • Harder to keep clean

Polished or epoxy floor

  • Smooth, resilient, uniform surface
  • Resists spills, stains and abrasion
  • Low-maintenance, built to last
  • Suited to Florida's heat and humidity
