Our process

From first call to final coat.

A streamlined workflow that makes upgrading your floors simple and stress-free.

01

Consultation

Tell us about your space and how it is used. We discuss finish options for polished concrete or epoxy.

02

Evaluation

Every project starts with a thorough look at the existing slab and its condition.

03

Surface preparation

Proper prep ensures flawless adhesion and a clean, uniform finish.

04

Polish or coat

Our technicians polish the concrete or apply the epoxy system with high-grade materials.

05

Seal and topcoat

The final seal or topcoat adds stain resistance, strength and the finish you chose.

06

Final walkthrough

We review the finished floor with you so you know it meets the standard we set.
