About us

Craftsmanship on site, careful management behind it

Affordable Remodeling provides residential construction throughout Metro Atlanta. We work with sensitivity to each client's needs, style and budget, and team with the city's best residential architects and designers to build homes around each family's requirements.

Before you sign a contract, you receive an extensive scope of services detailing all work to be performed, a schedule for completion and accurate pricing.

Why choose us

One owner, one reliable crew

Affordable Remodeling has built an impeccable team of reliable and honest subcontractors in the Atlanta building market. Hunt Warner, the owner, personally manages each renovation project from start to finish.

We are a member of NARI, the National Association of the Remodeling Industry, and have served the greater Atlanta market for over 15 years.
