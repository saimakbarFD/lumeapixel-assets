About Affordable Remodeling

Craftsmanship on the job site, management behind it

Our mission is to provide superior residential construction services throughout Metro Atlanta. We do it through job-site craftsmanship, professional project management and sensitivity to each client's needs, style and budget.

We team with Atlanta's best residential architects and designers to offer homes customized to each family. Before you sign, you get a full scope of services, a time schedule and an accurate price.

Why choose us

One owner, one trusted crew of subcontractors

Having worked the Atlanta building market for years, we have built a team of reliable, honest subcontractors. Hunt Warner, the owner, personally manages each renovation from start to finish.

We have proudly served greater Atlanta for over 15 years, and we are a member of NARI, the National Association of the Remodeling Industry.
