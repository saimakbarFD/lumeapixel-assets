About us

Talented, professional and experienced

Stress Free Construction is led by three construction-based entrepreneurs: David Lowrey, Chris Mercer and Jonathan Sands. With decades of combined background in construction, disaster recovery and property management, the owners of Stress Free Companies provide complete solutions.

Our project managers bring a wealth of experience and a real passion for building custom homes. We listen to your goals, your price range and your ideal location, then work with you to find the right home site and design the home that fits your budget.
