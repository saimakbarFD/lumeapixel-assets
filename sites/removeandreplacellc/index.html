FAQ

Questions, answered

What does a roofing inspection cost?+

The inspection is free. Our roof specialists assess your roof’s condition and identify issues early, so you can decide on repair or replacement with clear information.

Do you work with homeowners insurance claims?+

Yes. We are experts when it comes to roofing and insurance claims. Contact us for all of your roof repair or replacement needs.

Can you help after storm damage?+

We handle storm damage repair and respond quickly to restore your roof to its best condition.

Do you offer financing or ways to save?+

Our specialists look for ways to save you money through monthly specials and several financing options.

Do you only do roofing?+

No. Beyond roofing we install drywall, replace gutters, and handle siding, painting and remodeling for residential and commercial sites.

What roof types do you install?+

Shingle, PVC, TPO and metal roofing systems, for both homes and commercial buildings.
