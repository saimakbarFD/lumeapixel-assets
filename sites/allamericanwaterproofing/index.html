FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Call (404) 301-3431 and talk with our team.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. We offer free consultations and estimates, and we'll provide your free estimate in two or three days.

Do you stand behind your work?+

We back all of our services with a lifetime warranty. Our team is licensed and insured.

Will you match another company's price?+

We know cost matters, so we offer price matching with written quotes.

Are there discounts available?+

We offer a 10% discount to seniors, military personnel and first responders. Offers can't be combined with other promotions, and you must mention the discount when scheduling.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve Metro Atlanta and all of Georgia.

What kinds of waterproofing do you do?+

Basement, crawlspace, interior and exterior waterproofing, plus drainage solutions, retaining walls, sump pump maintenance and concrete services.
