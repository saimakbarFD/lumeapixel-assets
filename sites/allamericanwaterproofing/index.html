Questions, answered

How long has All-American Waterproofing been in business? +

Since 1961. We are a local, family-owned and veteran-owned business, with the same owner since the start.

Do you offer free estimates? +

Yes. Consultations and estimates are free, and we'll provide your estimate in two or three days.

Is your work warrantied? +

We stand by our work with a lifetime warranty on all services.

Do you match other quotes? +

Yes, we offer price matching with written quotes.

Are there discounts available? +

We offer 10% off to seniors, military personnel and first responders. Mention the discount when you schedule. It can't be combined with other offers or promotions, and restrictions apply.

Are you licensed and insured? +

Yes. Our team is licensed and insured, compliant with and certified by OSHA, and rated A+ by the BBB.
