About us

Six decades of keeping Georgia properties dry

Since 1961, All-American Waterproofing has been Metro Atlanta and Georgia's trusted name in waterproofing and concrete. It is a local, family-owned and veteran-owned business, and the owner has been the same since the start.

Water control is not only about keeping moisture out. It is about protecting the structure and giving you a stable foundation you do not have to worry about. Every job gets the same care and attention.

Our approach

Inside, outside and below the surface

When water finds its way in, we treat it from the inside with crack injections or full interior drainage. To stop it before it arrives, we seal the exterior, redirect runoff and fix the grade around your home.

We also perform outside excavation, so one crew can dig, waterproof, backfill and pour the concrete to finish the job.
