How do I know if I need a roof repair or a full replacement?+

If your roof is over 15 to 25 years old, has widespread damage, or you're dealing with recurring leaks, it may be time for a replacement. Smaller, isolated issues can often be repaired. The best way to know for sure is a professional inspection.

How long does a roof replacement take?+

Most residential roof replacements are completed in 1 to 3 days, depending on the size of the roof, materials and weather.

What types of roofing do you install?+

Asphalt shingles, architectural shingles, metal roofing and flat roofing systems.

What warranties do you offer?+

A 2-year warranty on repairs and a 10-year warranty on full roof replacements, plus manufacturer warranties on many materials.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. We inspect your roof, explain what we find, and walk you through options, timeline and pricing with no pressure.

Do you offer financing?+

Yes. Financing options are available. Call us or send a message and we will walk you through them.
