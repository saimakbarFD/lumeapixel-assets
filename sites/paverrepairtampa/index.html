FAQ

Straight answers to common questions

How much does paver repair cost in Tampa?+

Most residential repairs fall between a few hundred dollars for a small sunken section and a few thousand for a full driveway re-set. Every base, paver type and drainage situation is different, so we give a firm written price after a free on-site inspection.

How often should pavers be sealed in Florida?+

Most Tampa Bay pavers do well with resealing every 2 to 3 years given our sun and rain exposure. We will tell you honestly if yours can wait.

Why do my pavers keep sinking?+

Sinking is almost always a base or drainage issue underneath, not a problem with the pavers themselves. We diagnose the cause before repairing so it does not come back.

Can you match my existing pavers?+

In most cases yes. We assess the style, color and availability during your free inspection.

How long does a repair or restoration take?+

Most single-area repairs are done in a day. Larger jobs like a full driveway restoration, patio rebuild or pool deck typically take two to four days. We give you a clear timeline before work starts.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. Every estimate is free, with no obligation. You can also text a photo of the problem to (813) 767-0229 for a fast answer.
