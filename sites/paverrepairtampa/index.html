FAQ

Straight answers to common questions

Still unsure? Text or call (813) 767-0229 and we will help.

How much does paver repair cost in Tampa?+

Most residential repairs fall between a few hundred dollars for a small sunken section and a few thousand for a full driveway re-set. Because every base, paver type and drainage situation is different, we give a firm written price after a free on-site inspection.

How often should pavers be sealed in Florida?+

Most Tampa Bay pavers do well with resealing every 2 to 3 years given our sun and rain exposure. We'll tell you honestly if yours can wait.

Why do my pavers keep sinking?+

Sinking is almost always a base or drainage issue underneath, not a problem with the pavers themselves. We diagnose the cause before repairing so it doesn't come back.

Can you match my existing pavers?+

In most cases yes. We'll assess the style, color and availability during your free inspection.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. Every estimate is free, with no obligation.

How long does a paver repair or restoration take?+

Most single-area repairs are done in a day. Larger jobs like a full driveway restoration, patio rebuild or pool deck typically take two to four days depending on size and scope. We'll give you a clear timeline before work starts.

Does artificial turf get hot in Tampa?+

Modern turf runs warmer than grass in direct summer sun, but quality infill and proper installation keep it comfortable for most yards and pet areas.

Does restoring pavers actually add home value?+

Yes. Curb appeal and a finished outdoor living space are consistently cited by appraisers and buyers as value-adding features.
