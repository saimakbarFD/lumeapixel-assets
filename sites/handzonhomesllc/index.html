How do you approach project planning and execution?+

We start with an on-site consultation to understand your goals, budget and timeline. From there we create a clear scope of work and realistic schedule, and handle everything from permits to final walkthrough, keeping you informed every step of the way.

What types of projects do you specialize in?+

Residential remodeling and restoration, including kitchens, bathrooms, basements, home additions, decks and fire damage restoration. All photos on this site are real projects completed for homeowners in Metro Atlanta.

How long will my project take?+

Timelines vary by project type. Bathrooms typically take 2 to 3 weeks, kitchens 4 to 6 weeks, and basements 6 to 8 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline before work begins and keep you updated throughout.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes. Handz on Homes is fully licensed and insured for residential remodeling and restoration in Georgia, so your project meets local building and safety requirements.

How do you estimate costs and avoid surprises?+

We provide detailed written estimates based on your project scope. Pricing, options and potential changes are discussed upfront, so there are no surprises once work begins.

Do you handle permits and inspections?+

Yes. We prepare documentation, submit permits and coordinate all required inspections for applicable projects such as kitchens, bathrooms, basements and additions.

What areas do you serve?+

Homeowners in Atlanta, Roswell, Alpharetta, Sandy Springs, Stockbridge, McDonough and other surrounding Metro Atlanta communities. If you are unsure whether you are in our service area, just ask.
