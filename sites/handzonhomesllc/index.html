Questions

Before you call

How long will my project take?+

Bathrooms typically take 2 to 3 weeks, kitchens 4 to 6 weeks and basements 6 to 8 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline before work begins and keep you updated throughout.

Do you handle permits and inspections?+

Yes. We prepare documentation, submit permits and coordinate all required inspections for applicable projects such as kitchens, bathrooms, basements and additions.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes. Handz on Homes is licensed and fully insured for residential remodeling and restoration in Georgia.

How do you estimate costs?+

We provide detailed written estimates based on your project scope. Pricing, options and potential changes are discussed upfront so there are no surprises once work begins.

What areas do you serve?+

Atlanta, Roswell, Alpharetta, Sandy Springs, Stockbridge, McDonough and other surrounding Metro Atlanta communities. If you are unsure whether you are in our area, just ask.
