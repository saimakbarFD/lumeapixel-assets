About Punch

A contractor property managers keep calling back.

Punch Renovation & Construction works across Atlanta for homeowners, realty companies and commercial property managers. Our clients describe the crew as professional and courteous, with quality that meets or beats what was promised.

From roof repairs on apartment buildings to restoring a historic home after a fallen tree, we treat each property as if our name is on it. Because it is.
