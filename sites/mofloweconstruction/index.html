Safety

With an EMR safety rating of 0.73, safety is a core value that guides our decisions and actions. A safe workplace is a productive workplace, and every person on our crew should be able to do their job without risk of harm.

Our story

A highly skilled team dedicated to the most efficient, safe and reliable concrete services.

Our founder was introduced to construction the day he was born, riding along with his father in semi-trucks. Twenty years later, Flowe Construction is a midsize company employing people who share the same enthusiasm and drive to leave a lasting impact.
