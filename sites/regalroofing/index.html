Inspection

Regal's 20-point roof inspection.

A complete inspection leaves no detail overlooked. We check every critical component and flag damage that could lead to costly repairs later.

Structure

  • Roof decking condition
  • Attic ventilation
  • Roofing underlayment
  • Flashing integrity
  • Chimney condition
  • Skylight seals and frames

Surface

  • Shingle damage
  • Granule loss
  • Moss and algae growth
  • Nail pops and loose fasteners
  • Roof edge and drip edge

Drainage

  • Gutter system condition
  • Downspouts and water flow
  • Soffit and fascia

Interior and details

  • Interior ceiling and walls
  • Roof penetrations (vents, pipes, HVAC)
  • Ice dam prevention

Assessment

  • Storm damage inspection
  • Roof lifespan assessment
  • Warranty and maintenance review

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I tell if my roof is damaged after a storm?+

Sometimes the damage is obvious, like missing shingles, hail dents or leaks inside your home. Often it is hidden and not visible from the ground. Even small issues like lifted shingles or minor cracks can cause serious problems if not addressed early, so it is best to have a professional inspect your roof after a storm.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover a new roof?+

In many cases, yes, especially if the damage was caused by a storm. Regal Roofing works with insurance companies and can guide you through the entire claims process.

How much does a roof replacement cost?+

Cost depends on roof size, materials and the extent of the damage. If your roof qualifies for an insurance claim, your out-of-pocket cost may be minimal. We offer free inspections and estimates so you can make an informed decision.

How long does a roof replacement take?+

Most residential roof replacements are completed in one day. Our team works efficiently while keeping workmanship high and leaving your property clean and protected.

What roofing materials do you offer?+

We install asphalt shingles, metal roofing and flat roofing, all from top-rated manufacturers. We help you choose the best option for your home and budget.

Do you offer warranties on your roofing work?+

Yes. We back our work with labor warranties, and many roofing products also carry manufacturer warranties.
