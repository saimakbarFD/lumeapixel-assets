Questions

Roofing questions, answered.

Can I tell if my roof is damaged after a storm?+

Sometimes the damage is obvious, like missing shingles, hail dents or leaks inside. Often it's hidden from the ground. Even lifted shingles or minor cracks can cause serious problems if not addressed early, so have a professional inspect your roof after a storm.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover a new roof?+

In many cases, yes, especially if a storm caused the damage. We work with insurance companies and can guide you through the entire claims process.

How much does a roof replacement cost?+

Cost depends on roof size, materials and the extent of the damage. If your roof qualifies for an insurance claim, your out-of-pocket cost may be minimal. We offer free inspections and estimates so you can decide with the facts.

How long does a roof replacement take?+

Most residential roof replacements are completed in one day. We work efficiently, keep the quality high and leave your property clean and protected.

What roofing materials do you offer?+

Asphalt shingles, metal roofing and flat roofing from top-rated manufacturers. We help you choose based on your home's needs and your budget.

Do you offer warranties on your roofing work?+

Yes. We back our work with labor warranties, and many roofing products also carry manufacturer warranties.
