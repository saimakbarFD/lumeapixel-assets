FAQ

Questions homeowners ask.

Can I tell if my roof is damaged after a storm?+

Sometimes it is obvious, like missing shingles, hail dents or interior leaks. Often the damage is hidden and not visible from the ground. Even lifted shingles or minor cracks can cause serious problems later, so have a professional inspect your roof after a storm.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover a new roof?+

In many cases yes, especially when a storm caused the damage. We work with insurance companies and guide you through the whole claims process.

How much does a roof replacement cost?+

Cost depends on roof size, materials and the extent of damage. If your roof qualifies for an insurance claim, your out-of-pocket cost may be minimal. We offer free inspections and estimates.

How long does a roof replacement take?+

Most residential roof replacements are completed in one day, with quality workmanship and a clean, protected property.

What roofing materials do you offer?+

Asphalt shingles, metal roofing and flat roofing from top-rated manufacturers. We help you choose based on your home's needs and your budget.

Do you offer warranties on your work?+

Yes. We back our work with strong labor warranties, and many roofing products carry manufacturer warranties as well.
