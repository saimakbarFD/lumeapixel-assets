Inspection

Regal's 20-point roof inspection

A complete inspection is critical. We check every component so nothing is overlooked and damage is caught before it turns into a costly repair.

Structure

  • Roof decking condition
  • Attic ventilation
  • Roofing underlayment
  • Flashing integrity
  • Chimney condition
  • Skylight seals and frames

Surface

  • Shingle damage
  • Granule loss
  • Moss and algae growth
  • Nail pops and loose fasteners
  • Roof edge and drip edge

Water management

  • Gutter system condition
  • Downspouts and water flow
  • Soffit and fascia

Interior and details

  • Interior ceiling and walls
  • Roof penetrations (vents, pipes, HVAC)
  • Ice dam prevention

Assessment

  • Storm damage inspection
  • Roof lifespan assessment
  • Warranty and maintenance review

FAQ

Questions we hear often

Can I tell if my roof is damaged after a storm?+

Sometimes it is obvious: missing shingles, hail dents, or leaks inside. Often the damage is hidden and not visible from the ground. Lifted shingles or minor cracks can cause serious problems if left alone, so have a professional roofing company inspect your roof after a storm.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover a new roof?+

In many cases, yes, especially if a storm caused the damage. Regal Roofing works with insurance companies and can guide you through the whole claims process.

How much does a roof replacement cost?+

Cost depends on roof size, materials, and the extent of the damage. If your roof qualifies for an insurance claim, your out-of-pocket cost may be minimal. We offer free inspections and estimates so you can decide with the facts.

How long does a roof replacement take?+

Most residential roof replacements are completed in one day. We work efficiently while keeping the quality high and leaving your property clean and protected.

What roofing materials do you offer?+

We install asphalt shingles, metal roofing, and flat roofing from top-rated manufacturers, and help you pick what suits your home and budget.

Do you offer warranties on your roofing work?+

Yes. We back our work with strong labor warranties, and many roofing products carry manufacturer warranties as well.
