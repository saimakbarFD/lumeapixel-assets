FAQ

Common questions

Can I tell if my roof is damaged after a storm? +

Sometimes storm damage is obvious, like missing shingles, hail dents or leaks inside your home. Often it is hidden and not visible from the ground. Even small issues, like lifted shingles or minor cracks, can lead to serious problems if not addressed early, so it is best to have a professional inspect your roof after a storm.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover a new roof? +

In many cases, yes, especially if the damage was caused by a storm. We specialize in working with insurance companies and can help you through the entire claims process.

How much does a roof replacement cost? +

Cost varies with roof size, materials and the extent of the damage. If your roof qualifies for an insurance claim, your out-of-pocket cost may be minimal. We offer free inspections and estimates so you can make an informed decision.

How long does a roof replacement take? +

Most residential roof replacements are completed in one day. Our team works efficiently while keeping workmanship high and leaving your property clean and protected.

What roofing materials do you offer? +

We install asphalt shingles, metal roofing and flat roofing systems from top-rated manufacturers, and help you choose the best option for your home and budget.

Do you offer warranties on your roofing work? +

Yes. We back our work with strong labor warranties, and many of our roofing products carry manufacturer warranties as well.
