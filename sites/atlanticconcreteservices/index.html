About us

Strong concrete starts with careful prep

Good concrete is decided before the truck arrives. Grade, forms, reinforcement and timing all shape how a slab holds up for years. Atlantic Concrete Services puts the work in up front so it shows in the finished surface.

We serve homeowners, builders and businesses in and around Charlotte, North Carolina. Tell us what you are planning and we will walk through the scope, the schedule and the price in plain language.

How we work

On site, on schedule, on the details

Every job starts with a walk-through and a written estimate. From there we prepare the site, set forms and steel, place and finish the concrete, and clean up when we are done.
