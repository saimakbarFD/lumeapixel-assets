After the repair

Keep your roof in shape

A good repair lasts longer with a little upkeep. Here is what we tell Tampa homeowners.

Keep gutters clean

Clear leaves and twigs regularly so water drains away from your roof.

Trim overhanging trees

Branches over the roof can scrape and damage it. Cut them back.

Inspect regularly

Look for missing or damaged shingles and deal with them early.

Schedule inspections

A professional inspection catches small issues before they get expensive.
