FAQ

Questions, answered

What makes Alto the right choice for paving in Tampa?

We self-perform our work with our own equipment and personnel, backed by 40 years of experience. That lets us control the timeline and the final product, and meet every regulatory requirement on your commercial site.

Can you handle a full project, including the infrastructure?

Yes. We specialize in turn-key site development, including earthwork and underground utilities. We prepare the subgrade and install drainage systems before laying asphalt, so the infrastructure beneath your pavement is solid.

Do you offer maintenance after paving is complete?

Absolutely. We provide sealcoating, patching and overlays, and our crew handles striping and signage to keep your lot compliant with local regulations and handicap access requirements. Regular maintenance keeps small cracks from turning into costly structural failures.

Why does self-performing the work matter?

Self-performing 95% of our work gives us total control over schedule and material quality. Our own employees operate the machinery, which reduces communication errors, and you speak directly to the team doing the work rather than a middleman.

How does your recycling process help my budget?

Through our sister business we crush concrete and asphalt into sizes suitable for new construction. That reduces debris sent to landfills and gives you cost-effective base materials, such as crushed concrete for stabilized base work.

Do you work outside the Tampa Bay area?

Yes. Our roots are in Tampa, but over the last 40 years our reach has expanded to various Central Florida counties. Whether your project is in Orlando or Jacksonville, we bring our specialized equipment and experienced personnel to your location.
