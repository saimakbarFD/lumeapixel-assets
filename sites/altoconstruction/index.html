FAQ

Questions about paving in Tampa

Straight answers about our self-performed services, project management and four decades of site work.

What makes you a top choice among asphalt paving contractors in Tampa?+

We self-perform our work, using our own equipment and personnel for every phase. With 40 years of experience, we keep quality high without the delays that come with heavy subcontracting, and we control both the timeline and the final product so your site meets every regulatory requirement.

Can you handle a complete parking lot project, including the infrastructure?+

Yes. We specialize in turn-key site development, including earthwork and underground utilities. We prepare the subgrade and install drainage systems before laying asphalt, so the infrastructure beneath your pavement is solid and resists future sinkage or water damage.

What sets your team apart from other paving contractors?+

We are a full-service site development company. Beyond asphalt paving, we offer structural demolition and material recycling. We can clear a site, crush old materials for reuse and install storm drainage under one contract, which is more efficient than relying on third-party vendors.

Do you offer maintenance after the paving is complete?+

Yes. We provide sealcoating, patching and overlays, and our crew handles striping and signage to keep your lot compliant with local regulations and handicap access requirements. Regular maintenance keeps small cracks from turning into costly structural failures.

Why does self-performing work matter?+

Self-performing 95% of our work gives us total control over schedule and material quality. Our own employees operate the machinery, which reduces communication errors, and you speak directly to the people doing the work. It's why we've been a leading civil site contractor since 1984.

How does your recycling process help my budget and the environment?+

Our sister business crushes concrete and asphalt into sizes suited for new construction. This reduces debris sent to landfills and gives you cost-effective base materials, such as crushed concrete for stabilized base work, while streamlining the demolition phase.

What underground utility services do you provide?+

Our team is fully licensed for underground utility installation and excavation. We install storm drainage, water, sewer and fire lines before any paving begins. Handling these requirements ourselves removes extra contractors and keeps your project moving.

Do you work outside the Tampa Bay area?+

Yes. Our roots are in Tampa, but over 40 years our reach has expanded to various Central Florida counties. Whether your project is in Orlando or Jacksonville, we bring our specialized equipment and experienced personnel to your location.

How do I know when I need new parking lot paving?+

Look for widespread cracks, potholes, uneven areas or drainage problems. Standing water after heavy rain weakens the foundation, and Florida sun can fade and embrittle asphalt. Minor damage can be repaired with crack sealing or patching, but extensive alligator cracking, recurring potholes, crumbling pavement and large sunken areas usually call for resurfacing or complete repaving. We can inspect the lot and recommend what offers the best long-term value.
