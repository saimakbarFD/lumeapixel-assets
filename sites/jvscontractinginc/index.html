About JVS Contracting

Founded by John V. Simon, Jr.

JVS was established in 2005 by John V. Simon, Jr., who brings over 50 years of construction experience. We process over 600 thousand tons of concrete a year, and the material is 100% reusable.

We hold General Contractor, Plumbing Contractor, Pollutant Storage System Contractor, and Underground Utility and Excavation Contractor licenses.

  • Excellent safety record
