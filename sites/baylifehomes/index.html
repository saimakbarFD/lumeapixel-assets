Building for South Tampa

We don't just build in South Tampa. We live here.

With over 25 years in Tampa's real estate and construction markets, Bay Life Homes is deeply connected to the neighborhoods we serve. Our homes are designed for Florida's climate and lifestyle, crafted with block and steel and finished with the finest materials.

Every Bay Life home is fully finished and move-in ready, with smart home automation, premium appliances, impact-rated windows, custom cabinetry and designer landscaping. No upgrades needed. No decisions left unfinished.

Why Bay Life

A handful of projects each year, with owners involved throughout.

We intentionally take on only a few projects a year. That keeps the owners fully involved at every stage, from land acquisition to the final walk-through, so each home meets our standards for quality, detail and timeliness.

We work with trusted local subcontractors, craftsmen, engineers and suppliers, many with us since the beginning. That supports the Tampa Bay economy and gives us access to specialty products and high-quality materials. No cookie-cutter construction: every home is original and rich in detail.
