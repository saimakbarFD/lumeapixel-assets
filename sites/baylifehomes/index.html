Building for South Tampa

We don't just build in South Tampa. We live here.

With over 25 years in Tampa's real estate and construction markets, we know the neighborhoods we serve. Our homes are designed for Florida's climate and lifestyle, crafted with block and steel, and finished with the finest materials.

Every Bay Life Home is fully finished and move-in ready, with smart home automation, premium appliances, impact-rated windows, custom cabinetry and designer landscaping.

Our approach

A handful of projects each year, with owners on every one

We intentionally take on only a few projects a year. That keeps us, as owners, involved at every stage, from land acquisition to the final walk-through, so each home meets our standards for quality, detail and timeliness.

We work with trusted local subcontractors, craftsmen, engineers and suppliers, many of whom have been with us since the beginning. Every home is original, never cookie-cutter, and our local connections give us access to specialty products and quality materials.
