The Southerlyn difference

Family owned. Built on faith, integrity and craft.

2026 Parade of Homes winner

4311 W. Sevilla, South Tampa

Southerlyn Homes was founded by husband and wife Brett and Ashley Russell, who walk every client through each step of design and construction. Brett is a licensed contractor with over 10 years of residential building experience across South Tampa, St Petersburg, Belleair Beach, Seminole, Riverview, Redington Shores and Odessa. Ashley is a licensed Realtor with over 10 years of business and finance experience.

The name honors their Southern roots, their favorite style to build, Southern Coastal, and their oldest daughter, Rozalynn.
