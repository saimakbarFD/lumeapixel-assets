Family owned and operated

Meet Brett and Ashley Russell

Southerlyn Homes was founded by husband and wife team Brett and Ashley Russell. They have a passion for working with clients and walking them through every step of the design and construction process.

Brett is a Licensed Contractor with over 10 years of residential building experience. Before founding Southerlyn Homes, he built and renovated homes in South Tampa, St Petersburg, Belleair Beach, Seminole, Riverview, Redington Shores and Odessa. Ashley is a licensed Realtor and brings over 10 years of business and finance experience.

The name Southerlyn represents Brett and Ashley's Southern roots and favorite style of home to build, Southern Coastal, as well as a tribute to their oldest daughter, Rozalynn.

Questions, answered

Can you build on my lot? +

Yes. We build on your lot and turn your vision into reality with a full-scale team of architects, designers, engineers and artisans.

Who will I work with? +

Southerlyn Homes is family owned and operated. Brett Russell, a Licensed Contractor with over 10 years of residential building experience, and his wife Ashley walk clients through every step of the design and construction process.

Where do you build? +

Across Tampa Bay. Brett has built and renovated homes in South Tampa, St Petersburg, Belleair Beach, Seminole, Riverview, Redington Shores and Odessa.

What style of home do you build? +

Southern Coastal is our favorite, but every home is one of a kind and personalized to the way you live, from timeless coastal design to sleek modern architecture.

How do I get an estimate? +

Call (813) 600-0970, email team@southerlynhomes.com, or use the contact form below. Past clients describe the estimate process as quick and easy.

Can I see your current projects? +

Yes. We post regular construction updates and photos of finished work on Facebook and Instagram.
