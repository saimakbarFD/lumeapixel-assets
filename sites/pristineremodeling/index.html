Questions, answered

Where do you work? +

We remodel homes in Tampa, Clearwater, Riverview, Brandon, Valrico, Apollo Beach, St. Pete, Lutz and Zephyrhills.

Can you work in a condo or HOA community? +

Yes. Our team has experience working within condo associations and HOA guidelines, including approval processes, building access and scheduling, noise and work-hour restrictions, material and plumbing considerations, and coordination with property management.

Can I get a price range before committing? +

Photos and project details help us understand your goals and let us provide an initial ballpark pricing range. Consultations are no-obligation.

Are you insured? +

Yes, we are highly experienced and insured for your peace of mind.

Where can I look at finishes and plan my project? +

Our Tampa Bay office is at 610 W Horatio St., Suite 1, Tampa, FL 33606, off Bayshore Drive. It's a comfortable space to explore ideas, compare finishes and plan your project.
