FAQ

Questions, answered

Where is your Tampa Bay office? +

610 W Horatio St., Suite 1, Tampa, FL 33606, off Bayshore Drive. It's a space to explore ideas, compare finishes and plan your project.

Which areas do you serve? +

Tampa, Clearwater, Riverview, Brandon, Valrico, Apollo Beach, St. Pete, Lutz and Zephyrhills.

Do you work in condos and HOA communities? +

Yes. Our team has experience with condo associations and HOA guidelines, including approval processes, building access and scheduling, noise and work-hour restrictions, material and plumbing considerations, and coordination with property management.

Can you give me a price before I commit? +

Photos and project details help us understand your goals and let us provide an initial ballpark pricing range. Consultations are no-obligation.

Do you handle commercial projects? +

Yes. We offer commercial remodeling, commercial painting, tenant improvements and facility improvements.

Are you insured? +

We are highly experienced and insured for your peace of mind, and we treat your home like it's our own.
