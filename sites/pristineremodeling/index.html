FAQ

Questions, answered.

Planning a remodel in Tampa? Here is what homeowners ask us most.

Where is the Pristine office? +

Our Tampa Bay office is at 610 W Horatio St., Suite 1, Tampa, FL 33606, off Bayshore Drive. It is a comfortable space to explore ideas, compare finishes and plan your project.

Do you work in condos and HOA communities? +

Yes. Our team has experience working within condo associations and HOA guidelines, including approval processes, building access and scheduling, noise and work-hour restrictions, material and plumbing considerations, and coordination with property management.

Can I get a price range before committing? +

Photos and project details help us understand your goals and allow us to provide an initial ballpark pricing range. Consultations are no-obligation.

Which areas do you serve? +

Tampa, Clearwater, Riverview, Brandon, Valrico, Apollo Beach, St. Pete, Lutz and Zephyrhills.

What kind of work do you take on? +

Bathroom and kitchen remodeling, flooring and tile, cabinetry and storage, cabinet, interior and exterior painting, plus commercial remodeling, commercial painting and tenant improvements.

Are you insured? +

We are highly experienced and insured for your peace of mind, and we always treat your home like it’s our own.
