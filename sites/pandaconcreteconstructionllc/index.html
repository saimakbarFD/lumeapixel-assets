FAQ

Questions, answered

What types of concrete services do you offer? +

We provide driveways, patios, foundation slabs and decorative concrete options. Our work covers both residential and commercial projects.

How can I request a quote for my concrete project? +

Call us at 203-520-7079 to discuss your project details and we will provide a competitive estimate. You can also email pandaoutdoorrenovations@gmail.com.

What areas do you serve? +

We primarily serve the greater Tampa, Florida area and can accommodate projects in surrounding neighborhoods and cities. Contact us if you are unsure whether we can service your location.

What is your process for starting a new project? +

It begins with a phone consultation where we assess your needs and make recommendations. After we agree on scope and pricing, we schedule a time for our team to start and keep you updated throughout construction.
