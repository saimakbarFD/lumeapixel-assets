Questions, answered

Not seeing yours? Call (813) 344-0089 or send us a message.

What does Dorado Construction build?+

We are a full service general contractor specializing in custom homes, home additions, and renovations and remodels.

Can you build if I haven't bought a lot yet?+

Yes. Whether you already own your lot or are still in the process of acquiring one, our team is here to help you design and build your ideal home.

What do your pre-construction services include?+

Pre-construction services include site analysis, plan design, and cost and timeframe estimates.

Why choose a home addition?+

A home addition preserves the value of the existing structure while adding needed space and amenities. Our team will help you determine the feasibility, estimated cost and timeframe.

What is the difference between a renovation and an addition?+

A renovation or remodel maintains the existing footprint of the home while replacing its obsolete and outdated parts. An addition adds new space to the home.
